## Description (paste into the editor)

Looks easy. It is not.

**Kapiroto** is a tiny, mean platformer for the **MSX1** home computer (1983 hardware, TMS9918 video chip, 32 KB ROM). Walk to the green door. That's the whole game. Twenty rooms stand in your way, and every single one of them is lying to you.

- The floor crumbles when you step on it.
- Spikes pop out of the ground right where you wanted to land.
- Saw blades patrol the floor and the air.
- Spikes fall from the ceiling as you walk by.
- A block slides along the floor and shoves you into the hole.
- Platforms run away every time you jump.
- Even the exit door runs from you.

Every room can be beaten (they were all verified by a solver), but not on the first try. Expect to die a lot. Your death counter is always on screen.

**Controls**
- Left / Right arrows (or A / D): walk
- Space: jump
- R: restart the room, P: pause, M: sound on/off
- Joystick port 1: direction to walk, button A or B to jump

**How to play**
Kapiroto is a ROM for the MSX1 standard. Run it in an emulator:
- openMSX (Windows / macOS / Linux): `openmsx -machine C-BIOS_MSX1 -cart kapiroto.rom`
- WebMSX in the browser (webmsx.org): drag and drop `kapiroto.rom` onto the page.
It has been tested in openMSX with the free C-BIOS MSX1. It is a plain 32 KB ROM (no mapper), so it should also run from a flash cartridge on real MSX1 hardware, but that has not been tested.
